# Workstream 2: Internal Sales Tool

Gav's initial read: an **internal tool for Poolside Cairns' sales team**, with the idea that it could later expand to cover other things beyond the first use case. This still needs a proper conversation with them before scoping — see the open questions in `01-scope-and-brief.md`.

## Discovery questions to ask Poolside Cairns

- Walk me through a typical sales conversation from a customer's first inquiry to close — where does it slow down or get manual?
- What do salespeople currently look up or calculate by hand (pricing, specs, delivery lead times, financing options)?
- Do they want something used in-showroom on a tablet/laptop, or something their team can access from anywhere?
- Any systems it needs to work alongside — spreadsheets, a CRM, a booking/job system?
- What's the "other things" they mentioned wanting to expand into? (worth asking directly rather than assuming)

## Possible directions (to validate, not build yet)

- A spec/pricing lookup tool — quick reference for models, options, and current pricing so sales staff don't need to hunt through PDFs
- A simple quote builder — customer picks a model + options, gets a printable/emailable quote
- A comparison tool for customers — similar to a "which spa/sauna suits you" selector, usable on the showroom floor or website

## Notes

- This is Gav's own independent project for Poolside Cairns — keep the build and any tooling/accounts separate from Brisbane Spa Super Centre's systems.
- Keep this lightweight until scope is confirmed — no build work until the discovery questions above are answered.
